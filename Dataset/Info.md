

### Dataset Information

**Dataset Name:** Company Bankruptcy Prediction



**Overview:**
This dataset contains financial information of companies collected from the **Taiwan Economic Journal (TEJ)** between **1999 and 2009**. It is designed to help predict whether a company is likely to go bankrupt based on its financial performance and business indicators. Bankruptcy status is determined according to the regulations of the Taiwan Stock Exchange. ([Kaggle][1])

### Dataset Statistics

* **Total Records:** 6,819 companies
* **Total Features:** 96 columns

  * **95 Input Features:** Financial ratios and performance indicators
  * **1 Target Variable:** `Bankrupt?`

    * **1** = Bankrupt
    * **0** = Non-Bankrupt ([Kaggle][1])

### Features Included

The dataset contains a wide range of financial indicators, including:

* Return on Assets (ROA)
* Operating Gross Margin
* Net Profit Ratio
* Cash Flow Metrics
* Debt Ratio
* Current Ratio
* Quick Ratio
* Working Capital
* Revenue Per Share
* Earnings Per Share (EPS)
* Asset Growth Rate
* Inventory Turnover
* Interest Coverage Ratio
* Financial Leverage
* Equity-to-Liability Ratio
* And many other financial performance metrics. ([Kaggle][1])

### Target Variable

The **`Bankrupt?`** column is the output variable used for prediction:

* **0** → Company is financially healthy.
* **1** → Company is bankrupt. ([Kaggle][1])
